# speac Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 17, 2026

speac is a speaking-practice app for iPhone. There is no account, no cloud login, and no ads.

## What stays on your iPhone

When you tap the microphone, Apple’s speech recognition transcribes your practice. The transcript, scores, and notes are saved in this app’s local storage on your device only.

## What we don’t do

We don’t upload your voice or transcript to a speac server. We don’t sell data. We don’t show ads. We don’t use analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Notifications

Daily reminders use the system notification permission you grant. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove sessions, streaks, and custom prompts from this device.
